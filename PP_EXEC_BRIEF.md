## Most exciting capabilities from a PP-exec POV (honest assessment)

**1. The signal-vs-coverage gap — "what's real but under-covered."**
This is the capability with no off-the-shelf equivalent. Guttmacher/KFF tell you the current map; POLITICO tells you the story everyone's already reading. *Nothing* tells you **"this is moving hard and no one's watching."** For a resource-constrained org that must pick its fights, a ranked list of *under-covered* threats (personhood, today) is directly actionable — it reallocates attention *ahead* of the press cycle. This is the single most exec-relevant output.

**2. Cross-feed corroboration across the state + federal + court lanes.**
A threat lit in *four independent feeds* (mifepristone) is a fundamentally different confidence level than a single-source rumor. Because the net spans 50 legislatures + federal dockets + agencies at once, it sees the *pattern* — e.g., a copycat model-bill campaign across AZ/IN/OK/SC — that no affiliate watching its own state can see. It augments the expert's bandwidth exactly where a human can't scale.

**3. Long-lead structural warning from bills at introduction.**
The §71113 re-pass threat is already visible at **47%** from a bill *introduction* (S.203) months before any rule — the entire point of early warning. The tool converts "we got blindsided" into "we flagged this in Q1."

*Round-4 hardening (measured):* the state-exclusion gate was tightened (co-occurrence + omnibus-negative + relevance floor) — **precision 0.82 → 1.00, F1 0.90 → 0.97 on a hand-labeled golden set**; the NY omnibus-budget false positives are gone. Personhood routing now strips corporate/AI/anti-personhood bills. A **keyless Tier-0 state-court monitor** now covers the shield-law/Carpenter gap via case-name news watching (WV abortion-pill ruling, PA Medicaid, SisterSong-GA, MO Amendment 3 all captured live).

*Honest limits an exec should know:* **news volume is noisy and saturates**, so the gap metric is **directional, not precise**; the Tier-0 state-court feed catches *newsworthy* rulings but **misses routine docket entries** (true docket-level tracking needs a paid API or the co-counsel intake in `STATE_COURT_SOP.md`); stance detection under-tags some pro-access bills. Trust the corroborated, mechanistic signals; treat single-feed and news-only items as leads to verify.
