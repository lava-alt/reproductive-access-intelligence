Why this matters, capabilities for PP leadership

Signal vs. coverage gap

What is real but under-covered. No off-the-shelf tool does this. Guttmacher shows the current map, POLITICO shows the story everyone is already reading. Only this flags moving hard, no one watching. It reallocates attention ahead of the press cycle, and today it points at personhood.

A threat lit in four independent feeds (mifepristone) is a different confidence class than a single-source rumor. Spanning 50 legislatures, federal dockets, and agencies at once, it sees the pattern (a copycat campaign across AZ, IN, OK, SC) that no single-state watcher can.

Sharper with your own case data

The one blind spot is data you already hold.

Everything above runs on public signal alone. The single gap named in the limits below, routine state-court docket activity, is exactly the information Planned Parenthood already has internally: the cases co-counsel are actively litigating, the legal intake pipeline, affiliate court calendars. Feeding that live case list into the same model turns a blind spot into an advantage no outside tool can match.

  • Closes the docket gap at zero cost. No paid API needed. The filings and motions come straight from your own matters, so the tool sees them the day they happen, not when they reach the press.
  • Adds proprietary lead time. Your own new filings and emergency motions become early signals, weeks ahead of any public source, because you are the source.
  • Builds richer trends. Linking the legislative wave to the litigation it triggers lets a copycat bill campaign and the suits that follow show up as one connected trend instead of two separate signals. More ground truth, sharper patterns.
  • Lets the model learn from outcomes. Which flagged threats actually became active cases is the label the weights train on, so calibration improves the longer it runs beside your caseload.

All of it stays internal. The tool runs on your side. Nothing about active matters is ever hosted or shared.

Honest limits an exec should know: news volume is noisy and saturates, so the gap metric is directional, not precise. The keyless state-court feed catches newsworthy rulings but misses routine docket entries (true docket tracking needs a paid API such as Trellis at roughly $70 to $120 per month, or the co-counsel intake in STATE_COURT_SOP.md). Stance detection under-tags some pro-access bills. Trust the corroborated, mechanistic signals, treat single-feed and news-only items as leads to verify.
